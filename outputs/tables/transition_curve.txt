STEP OR RAMP? Boundary jump against the within-game pre-boundary trend
================================================================================================
The pre-boundary slope is fitted on positions -5..-1, weighted by
cell size. It is a WITHIN-GAME baseline for how much thinking time
rises per move with no boundary crossed. The ply-trend probe
estimated this cross-sectionally; this is the direct measurement.

band        pre-slope   predicted    observed    excess    ratio
----------------------------------------------------------------
band_1       -0.02694    -0.02694     0.23064   0.25759      inf
band_2       -0.03092    -0.03092     0.25628   0.28720      inf
band_3       -0.03229    -0.03229     0.26306   0.29536      inf
band_4        0.00611     0.00611     0.29937   0.29326     49.0
band_5        0.03843     0.03843     0.35708   0.31865      9.3

pre-slope  = log-seconds gained per move while still in book
predicted  = what the -1 -> 0 step would be if the trend simply continued
observed   = the actual -1 -> 0 step
excess     = observed minus predicted, i.e. the boundary-specific part
ratio      = observed / predicted

CAVEAT ON THE PRE-SLOPE. It is fitted across positions -5..-1, but
those cells contain different players: only ~17% of player-games
reach -5, and those are the ones who stayed in book longest.
Validation on synthetic data with a known trend showed this biases
the fitted slope LOW (recovering 0.010-0.013 where the true value
was 0.020), which biases the ratio HIGH. On synthetic data built as
a pure ramp with no boundary effect at all, the ratio still read
1.6-1.7x rather than 1.0. Treat ratios below roughly 2x as
consistent with no boundary-specific effect.

Smallest ratio across bands: 9.3x
The jump is several times the within-game trend in every band.
This is a STEP, not a continuation of a ramp.

HOW TO REPORT THIS
================================================================================================
1. This is exploratory. Label it as such in its own section,
   separate from the H1-H3 results, and state that it was
   formulated after the confirmatory results were observed.
2. The pre-boundary slope here is a within-game measurement and
   supersedes the cross-sectional ply-trend probe. Report both,
   and say which is which.
3. Report cell counts with every estimate. The panel is
   unbalanced by design: ~16.6% coverage at -5 against 100% at
   -1 and 0. Do not present the curve as if all positions were
   equally well estimated.
4. If the ratio is small, that is a real qualification to H1 and
   belongs in the abstract, not only in a limitations paragraph.
