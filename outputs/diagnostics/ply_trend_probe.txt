The boundary jump exceeds the ply trend, but not by a wide
margin in every band. This is a limitation, and the full
transition curve is needed.

CAVEAT: this trend is cross-sectional. Games leaving book at ply 30
may differ systematically from games leaving at ply 10, so the slope
conflates "later plies are slower" with "longer-book games differ".
It bounds the concern; it does not resolve it. Only a within-game
window (moves -5 to +10) measures the actual trend.
